Questions people ask

Is Tito free?

Yes, and the code is open. The AI keys you paste come with their own free tiers from Gemini and Groq.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Download the offline packs once and Tito explains and translates English and Hindi on your PC. Meetings, Excel, PDF tools, notes and exam plans work without the internet anyway.

Does my screen get uploaded?

Only the part you select goes to the AI you chose, nothing else. In offline mode nothing leaves your PC. Keys are encrypted on your machine.

What does Focus Guard see?

Only the title and program name of the window in front, every few seconds during a focus round. It stays on your PC and only a short summary is kept.

Which languages can it translate?

Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Urdu and more online. Offline covers English and Hindi.

Where do my files go?

Excel sheets, PDFs, notes and calendar files are saved in Documents\Tito on your PC, and a button opens them.

Mac or Linux?

Windows only for now.
