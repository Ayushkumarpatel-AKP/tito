  2. 2

    Run it

Questions people ask

Is Tito free?

Yes, and the code is open. The AI keys you paste come with their own free tiers from Gemini and Groq.

Does my screen get uploaded?

Only the part you select goes to the AI you chose, nothing else. In offline mode nothing leaves your PC. Keys are encrypted on your machine.

Which languages can it translate?

Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Urdu and more online. Offline covers English and Hindi.

How big is it?

About 350 MB installed. The offline packs are optional and download separately.

Mac or Linux?

Windows only for now.
