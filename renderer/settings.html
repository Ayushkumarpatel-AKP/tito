How Tito thinks

AI provider bring any key, Tito picks the best model

Paste a key from any supported service and Tito recognises it, finds the newest image-capable model that works and tests it with a small picture. If a model is ever retired, Tito finds a replacement on its own. With several keys saved, Tito switches provider automatically when one is busy or out of free quota.

Translation

MyMemory is free and needs no key. It is quick and covers all major Indian languages, but its quality varies, so the AI is the default. Offline translation covers English and Hindi.

Offline packs downloaded once, kept on this PC

Without a pack, offline mode still reads your selection with the text reader built into Windows. Packs are optional, so the installer stays small.

Memory

Tito keeps the titles of your recent questions so follow-ups and answers stay relevant. It is stored only on this PC and never uploaded except as a short note inside your AI request.

Tito's voice and ringtone

The free natural voice uses your Gemini key (free tier). Any other service works with its own key: OpenAI, ElevenLabs, or an OpenAI-compatible address. Without a key, Tito can use the built-in Windows voice, which sounds robotic.

Dock, voice and search

Focus Guard only looks at the title of the window in front, on this PC, during a focus round. Nothing is saved except the summary, and nothing is sent anywhere. A YouTube lecture or notes do not count as distractions.

Voice commands (the mic in the dock) use your free Groq key from the AI provider card above. Audio is sent to Groq only while you use the mic.
