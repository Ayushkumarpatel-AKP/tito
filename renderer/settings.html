How Tito thinks

AI provider bring any key, Tito picks the best model

Paste a key from any supported service and Tito recognises it, finds the newest image-capable model that works and tests it with a small picture. If a model is ever retired, Tito finds a replacement on its own. With several keys saved, Tito switches provider automatically when one is busy or out of free quota.
